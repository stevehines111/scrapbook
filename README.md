Scrapbook: lay out your own photos on a 13 × 19, 8.5 × 11 or 4 × 6 sheet and print them at exact size. Photos never leave your computer.
Live: https://stevehines111.github.io/scrapbook/
Run locally: open `index.html` in a browser, or run `python -m http.server` in this folder and visit http://localhost:8000.
